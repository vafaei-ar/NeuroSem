## Writing rules

1. Keep neural-geometry replication, neural-guided model alignment, and external transfer as separate claims.
2. Preserve TMNRED, Garnett, Nature, and AHBA null findings rather than presenting only the positive ZuCo result.
3. Label analyses as confirmatory/frozen, exploratory, or post-hoc diagnostic consistently with `docs/4_EXPERIMENT_LEDGER.md`.
4. Treat AHBA as a six-donor population postmortem spatial prior, not participant-specific molecular measurement.
5. Do not promote the no-mirror dyslexia-panel sensitivity to confirmatory evidence; its role is a bilateral-processing diagnostic that motivates future independent validation.
6. Before submission, link every reported number to its final figure/table source, exact RunRelay job, project commit, and canonical safe artifact.

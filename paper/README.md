## Evidence architecture

The primary prospective chain remains ChineseEEG development, independent ZuCo EEG transfer, and prospective SMN4Lang fMRI transfer after a model-blind reliability gate. Subsequent specificity, seed, dose, reverse-transfer, model-family, model-space, boundary, regional, and spatial analyses are reported as post-confirmatory characterization rather than as additional primary tests.

The manuscript explicitly preserves negative and limiting evidence, including DERCo negative transfer, the failed SMN4Lang MEG reliability gate, model-family heterogeneity, and the structured-surrogate magnitude mismatch.

## Writing constraints

Reliability, learnability, development selection, and external transfer remain distinct empirical stages. The forward and reverse dose series are post-confirmatory. Model-family analyses do not establish a causal architecture mechanism or E5 uniqueness. Regional fMRI results do not establish language-network specificity. The failed MEG reliability gate is not negative transfer. Raw EEG and fMRI RSA values are not interpreted on a common effect-size scale.

No additional outcome-bearing analysis is required for the current submission package. Remaining work is limited to author metadata, journal production, reporting-summary completion, citation-manager refresh if needed, and archival release preparation.
